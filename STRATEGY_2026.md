# Drug Runner. Corp. — 2026 Draft Strategy

Built from five League UNC auctions (2021-2025, ~750 sales) joined to final
standings, plus league settings (10-team ESPN standard non-PPR, 15 spots,
$500 base / $560 for you, keepers +$15, room overpays elites ~15-25%).

## What actually wins this league (the data)

50 team-seasons, drafts joined to finishes:

| Metric | Champions | Middle (4-7) | Signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Biggest single buy | $127 avg | $139 | The trophy buy does NOT win |
| Top-3 spend share | 54.5% | 59.9% | Concentration hurts |
| Players ≥ $100 | 1.6 | 1.3 | ~same |
| **Players ≥ $60** | **3.6** | 2.7 | **The strongest winning signal (r = −0.26)** |
| Players ≤ $5 | 4.2 | 4.1 | Everyone punts the tail |
| QB spend share | 7% (r = −0.23) | 7% | Paying for a real QB correlates with winning |

- **No $190+ purchase has won the title in five years** (Omar's $192 Cook 2021 → 10th,
  Link's $199 Bijan 2023 → 8th, Byrd's $198 Bijan 2025 → 3rd).
- Every champion had **3-5 players in the $60-110 band** and exactly 1-2 over $100.
- **QBs are the market inefficiency**: top QB went $55-83 while top RBs went
  $157-199. Farmer just won it all with Josh Allen at $83 (15% of spend on QB —
  the highest QB share of any champion, and it worked).
- RB-heavy vs WR-heavy doesn't decide anything (r ≈ 0); *where the discount
  is* decides. 2023 champ was 62% RB; 2024 champ was 52% WR.

**≥ $120 still in hand** when ~90 players are gone — that's when this room's
budgets historically collapse and the $40 players go for $18.

## Room-specific tactics

- **Nominate elite RBs you don't want, immediately.** The premium is front-loaded:
  first-nominated elites clear value by 15-25%. Every $198 Bijan is $198 that
  can't bid against you later. Feed the fire; never fight it.
- **Price-enforce Lesesne** ($600, the only wallet bigger than yours; keeps
  Etienne $24 + Tee $87). His profile: pays for name brands. Make each cost $10 more.
- **Link is broke** ($400, minus a $120-125 keeper from Gibbs/JT → ~$280 for
  13 spots). He cannot fight you for anything mid-draft. Whatever he needs, wait him out.
- **Byrd ($440) keeps JSN at $68** — he'll have one big swing left. When he
  wins his elite, he's done; the seat after Byrd's big buy is a discount window.
- **Singer is the danger** (fewest trades, two rings, keeps Pickens $50 +
  McBride $65 = massive surplus). He drafts balanced and disciplined — don't
  expect gifts from him; just don't gift him either.
- **Farmer post-Allen-decision**: if he keeps Achane + Warren, he re-buys a QB —
  make him pay full price; if he keeps Allen, his depth is thin — outlast him.
- **Deep-sleeper endgame**: most teams finish drafting with $1-3 maximum bids by
  design; your last $30 vs their $5 wins every dart you actually want. Hold
  $25-35 for the final 15 nominations — it's a monopoly, not a budget error.
